Help and licenses

Keep your head steady and face the camera. Tracking stays on this device and does not control your dial yet.

Grant camera access briefly opens the default camera to reveal available webcams, including virtual cameras, then stops it.

Calibration shows 17 targets. Look at each target until it moves. Press Escape or Stop Camera to cancel.

Stabilization filters isolated spikes and holds small fluctuations still. Larger radii and Strong mode reduce shaking but add lag and hold-position offset. Lower Speed makes the circle glide more slowly toward where you look. Applies only to the green gaze indicator, not your mouse or clicks. During tracking loss, the circle holds its last known position; it is not a live estimate.

Gaze joystick: after calibration, a blue dot starts at the centre of the panel. Look away from the centre to push it in that direction; looking further away moves it faster. Looking inside the dashed dead zone, or losing tracking, stops it. Recenter returns it to the middle.
